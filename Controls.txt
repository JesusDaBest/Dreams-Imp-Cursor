Imp Carousel — mouse reactions

Move: native body/antenna motion and directional looks.
Screen edges: the native edge response acts on the body.
Left click / double click: selection reactions.
Left drag / hold / release: grab, pulling, hold and drop reactions.
Right click / hold: menu and menu-hold reactions.
Right/middle drag: movement reactions; release: drop.
Scroll: look up/down; horizontal scroll: look sideways.
Ctrl + scroll: resize reactions.
Temporary D-pad size calibration is off after setting the default size.
Mouse Back / Forward: undo / redo reactions.
Ctrl / Shift while dragging: clone/hold variants.
Hover an interactive Windows control: pointer glow, when detected by Windows
accessibility or the application's standard link cursor.
Text, busy, resize, move, unavailable, link, help and other standard Windows
cursor roles select their corresponding Dreams animation.
Idle: native common idle/blink reactions, subject to the chosen Imp's supported features.
Appearance-specific emote selectors retain their four original ordered slots.
Windows event mappings adapt native clips; they do not reproduce every game event.
Mouse cursor hidden by an application: the Imp hides too, until it is shown again.

Open Imp Carousel.exe and tap or click an Imp to set it as your cursor.
Drag the empty background or main preview to scroll, or use the mouse wheel.
Drag the header outside its buttons to move the window; dragging it to the screen's top edge uses
Windows' normal maximize/snap behavior. Window edges resize it.
All contents keep their proportions; non-16:9 windows have space around them.
F11 or Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen; Escape leaves fullscreen.
The category dropdown filters Imps without changing the active cursor.
The + button saves the selected Imp to the heart tab.
Bottom-row Imps idle until hovered, then play Dreams' custom-hover clip.
Selecting a row Imp changes the large preview without scrolling the row.
Closing the picker keeps the selected cursor running and releases its previews.
Right-click the Imp Carousel system-tray icon to reopen the picker or restore the
Windows cursor. Double-click the tray icon to open the picker.

Click the top-left Imp icon or title to choose menu music from your files.
A default song is bundled. Music loops while the picker is focused.
Click the top-centre Imp button once to select the carousel, then again for categories.

No carousel is shown until a top tab is selected.
A green check marks only the currently running Imp cursor. Click that Imp again to restore Windows.
Right-click the cursor tray icon and choose Exit to close the app and cursor together.
End Task on the app launcher also stops its renderer processes.
