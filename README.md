1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open **Dreams Imp.exe**.
3. Open a carousel with the top buttons, then click an Imp to use it as your cursor.

Click an Imp again to restore your Windows cursor. Close the carousel to keep
your chosen Imp running; right-click its tray icon and choose **Exit** to close
everything.

Explore the **Imp Carousel**, save your **favourites**, or try **Imp Surprise**
for a new combination of colours and features. **F11** toggles fullscreen.

Music is included. Click the top-left Imp icon or title to choose your own song.
