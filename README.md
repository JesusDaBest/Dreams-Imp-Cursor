# Dreams Imp Cursor

Give your cursor a little personality.

Bring Dreams’ Imps to your desktop—with expressive faces, movement and a live
carousel to find your favourite. Pick an Imp, make it yours, and take it anywhere
on Windows.

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/JesusDaBest/Dreams-Imp-Cursor/releases/latest/download/Dreams-Imp-Windows.zip)**

1. Download and extract the ZIP.
2. Open **Dreams Imp.exe**.
3. Click an Imp to use it as your cursor.

Close the carousel and your Imp stays with you. Its tray icon brings you back
or restores your Windows cursor.

Explore the **Imp Carousel**, save your **favourites**, or try **Imp Surprise**
for a new combination of colours and features. **F11** toggles fullscreen.

Windows 10/11 x64, AVX2 CPU and compatible Vulkan 1.3 graphics required.
