Imp Carousel for Windows — 1.1.4

Extract the complete ZIP, then double-click Imp Carousel.exe.
Click an Imp in the carousel to use it as your mouse cursor.
Everything the app needs is included: no commands, Python installation,
Dreams installation, emulator, account or additional asset downloads.

Closing the picker keeps your Imp cursor running and stops its previews.
Use the Imp Carousel system-tray icon to reopen the picker or restore the
normal Windows cursor. Opening the picker does not change your cursor.

The app opens fullscreen. F11 or Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen; Escape leaves
fullscreen. Drag to scroll the carousel. The + button saves the selected Imp
in the heart tab. Imp Surprise generates a separate carousel of appearances.

Includes 149 game presets and original animation data, native particle/motion
routines and ported game shaders. Windows actions drive the cursor reactions.
The fragment and temporal-resolve shaders use standard instructions instead of AMD-only trinary min/max.
Exact parity with every Dreams animation and event has not been established.

Requirements
Windows 10/11 x64, AVX2 CPU and a compatible Vulkan 1.3 graphics driver.
The renderer requires its reported subgroup, barycentric and shader features.
Tested locally on AMD Radeon 8060S. NVIDIA and Intel are not physically tested;
support depends on the GPU and driver features, not the brand name.
The app reports unavailable features and leaves the normal cursor available.
Secure desktops and exclusive fullscreen applications may restrict overlays.

Settings and logs are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%/Dreams Imp.
Your local settings and music files are not included in the public download.
The program does not need a server, install a service or change Dreams saves.
